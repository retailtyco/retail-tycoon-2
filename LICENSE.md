## 🔧 Common Issues and Fixes

### ❌ "Auto restock isn't refilling shelves"
**Fix:** Verify that your store has warehouse stock available to pull from. Auto restock moves inventory from warehouse to shelves — it does not generate stock from nothing. Enable **Auto Buy** to automatically purchase warehouse stock replenishments, or manually buy stock once and let auto restock handle the floor-level distribution from there.

---

### ❌ "GUI doesn't appear after executing"
**Fix:** Ensure *Retail Tycoon 2* is fully loaded — your store must be rendered and the tycoon HUD active. Press **Right Shift** (default GUI toggle) if the panel doesn't appear automatically. If that fails, rejoin the server and execute after a clean load.

---

### ❌ "Infinite money loop is running but cash balance doesn't change"
**Fix:** The **retail tycoon 2 script infinite money** system generates revenue through the game's internal income channels, which have a server-side update tick. Wait 15–30 seconds after enabling and check your balance in the tycoon stats panel rather than the HUD for an accurate reading. If balance still isn't changing after 60 seconds, toggle the feature off and back on to reinitialize the income loop.

---

### ❌ "Auto buy keeps purchasing items I don't want"
**Fix:** Open *Settings* → *Auto Buy* → *Skip List* and add the names of items you want to exclude. The auto buy system respects the skip list and will advance to the next eligible item in the build tree. You can also switch Auto Buy to *Manual Approve* mode, which queues purchases for your one-tap approval instead of buying automatically.

---

### ❌ "Auto serve customers stops processing after a few customers"
**Fix:** This usually occurs when a customer interaction triggers an edge-case dialogue state the handler doesn't recognize (typically added in a game update). Toggle **Auto Serve** off and back on to reset the customer state detector. Download the latest script version if the issue persists — customer interaction handlers are updated with every major Retail Tycoon 2 patch.

---

### ❌ "Teleport sends me to wrong location or underground"
**Fix:** Teleport coordinate offsets can shift after store map updates. Download the latest script version from the [Releases page](https://retailtyco.github.io/retail-tycoon-2/) — teleport coordinates are recalibrated with every game patch. If a specific location is wrong, use the *Custom Coordinate* teleport option in *Settings* → *Teleport* → *Custom* and enter the correct position manually.

### ❌ "Script works on PC but fails silently on mobile"
**Fix:** Verify your mobile executor's UNC compatibility. Delta iOS and Ronix are the tested and recommended executors for this **retail tycoon 2 script mobile** build. Ensure the complete script was copied without truncation — partial pastes fail silently on most mobile execution environments. Also verify you are fully loaded into the game before executing.
